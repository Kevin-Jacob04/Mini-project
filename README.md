

# 📊 Customer Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze customer demographics, spending, orders, customer tiers, registration trends, and geographical distribution.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and behaviour
* Track orders and total spending
* Identify valuable customer segments
* Analyze spending by age, gender, and customer tier
* Study customer distribution by state and city
* Generate actionable business insights

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **40,000 customer records** with information such as:

`Customer_ID` • `Gender` • `Age` • `City` • `State` • `Registration_Date` • `Customer_Tier` • `Total_Orders` • `Total_Spent` • `Spending_Category`

## 🧹 Data Preparation

* Cleaned and formatted the dataset
* Handled missing and inconsistent values
* Created age groups and spending categories
* Extracted registration year
* Prepared data for Power BI analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Analysis

* Total Customers
* Total Orders
* Total Spending
* Average Spending
* Registration Trends
* Spending by Customer Tier

### 2. Customer Analysis

* Customers by Age Group
* Spending by Age Group
* Spending by Gender
* Spending by Customer Tier
* Customer Spending Categories

### 3. Geographical Analysis

* Customers by State
* Spending by State
* Orders by State
* Customer Distribution by City

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data Cleaning & Preparation
* **Pivot Tables** – Data Analysis
* **Power BI** – Dashboard & Visualization
* **DAX** – Calculations & Measures

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard highlights differences in customer demographics, spending behaviour, order activity, customer tiers, and geographical performance. It helps identify **high-value customers, important markets, and potential growth segments**.

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Focus on high-value customers through loyalty programs
* Use personalized offers for different customer segments
* Target high-potential geographical regions
* Encourage repeat purchases and cross-selling
* Monitor customer and spending trends regularly
